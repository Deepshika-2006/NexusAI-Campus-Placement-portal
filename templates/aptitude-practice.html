Question 1 of 5Medium

A product is marked 25% above its cost price and sold at a 10% discount on the marked price. What is the profit percentage?

Reveal answer & explanation

Answer: 12.5%. Take CP = 100. MP = 125. Selling price after 10% discount = 112.5, so profit = 12.5%.

Question 2 of 5Easy

The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2. If 20 girls are present, how many students are in the class?

Reveal answer & explanation

Answer: 50. Two ratio parts represent 20 girls, so one part is 10. Total parts = 5, giving 50 students.

Question 3 of 5Medium

A can complete a task in 12 days and B can complete it in 18 days. Working together, how many days will they take?

Reveal answer & explanation

Answer: 7.2 days. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36, therefore time = 36/5 = 7.2 days.

Question 4 of 5Medium

A bag contains 5 red, 3 blue and 2 green balls. What is the probability of drawing a blue ball?

Reveal answer & explanation

Answer: 3/10. There are 10 balls in total and 3 are blue, so probability = 3/10.

Question 5 of 5Easy

The average of five numbers is 28. If one number is removed, the average becomes 25. What is the removed number?

Reveal answer & explanation

Answer: 40. Original total = 5 × 28 = 140. Remaining total = 4 × 25 = 100. Removed number = 40.
